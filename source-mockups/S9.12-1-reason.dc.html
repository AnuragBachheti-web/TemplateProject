Why this is queued

Apparel returns are 1.6× the category benchmark, and most of it is one sentence customers keep writing

A return costs more than the refund. Inbound freight, inspection, repack and the markdown on what comes back add up to real money, and a third of it here traces to a fit problem we could fix on the page rather than in the warehouse. This story separates prevention from disposition: stop the return that should not happen, and recover more on the one that will.
